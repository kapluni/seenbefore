# Anti-Zionist Committee of the Soviet Public - Declaration (April 1, 1983)
# Source: Pravda, April 1, 1983. Compiled from Wikipedia, SAPIR Journal, and Marxists.org

## Full Declaration (as published in Pravda)

"By its nature, Zionism concentrates ultra-nationalism, chauvinism and racial intolerance, excuse for territorial occupation and annexation, military opportunism, cult of political promiscuousness and irresponsibility, demagogy and ideological diversion, dirty tactics and perfidy."

"Absurd are attempts of Zionist ideologists to present those who criticize them, or condemn the aggressive politics of Israel's ruling circles, as antisemitic."

"We call on all Soviet citizens: workers, peasants, representatives of intelligentsia: take active part in exposing Zionism, strongly rebuke its endeavors; social scientists: activate scientific research to criticize reactionary core of that ideology and aggressive character of its political practice; writers, artists, journalists: fuller expose anti-populace and anti-humane diversionary character of propaganda and politics of Zionism."

## From the SAPIR Journal version (Izabella Tabarovsky translation):

"In its essence, Zionism embodies extreme nationalism, chauvinism, and racial intolerance, justification of territorial conquests and annexations."

"Having kidnapped the right of 'defenders' of Soviet Jews, those Zionist wheeler-dealers try to persuade the world's public opinion that, allegedly, in the USSR there exists the 'Jewish question.'"

"It is with contempt that Soviet Jews cast aside the attempts by Zionist propagandists to interfere in their life."

## Context:
# Formation approved March 29, 1983 by CPSU Secretariat resolution 101/62
# Resolution text: "Support the proposition of the Department of Propaganda of the Central Committee and the KGB USSR about the creation of the Anti-Zionist Committee of the Soviet Public"
# Chairman: Colonel-General David Dragunsky (twice Hero of the Soviet Union)
# The committee was a KGB-established propaganda tool using prominent Jewish figures to deflect accusations of antisemitism
