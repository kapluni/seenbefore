# Great Soviet Encyclopedia - Entry on Zionism
# Source: Third edition (Большая Советская энциклопедия, БСЭ), published 1969-1978
# Compiled from documented quotes in Wikipedia and academic sources

## Official CPSU Definition of Zionism:

"The main posits of modern Zionism are militant chauvinism, racism, anti-Communism and anti-Sovietism."

"The anti-human reactionary essence of Zionism" is "overt and covert fight against freedom movements and against the USSR."

"International Zionist Organization owns major financial funds, partly through Jewish monopolists and partly collected by Jewish mandatory charities." It also "influences or controls significant part of media agencies and outlets in the West."

"Serving as the front squad of colonialism and neo-colonialism, international Zionism actively participates in the fight against national liberation movements of the peoples of Africa, Asia and Latin America."

"A natural and objective assimilation process of Jews is growing around the world."

## Official Position (from CPSU):
"The main posits of modern Zionism are militant chauvinism, racism, anti-Communism and anti-Sovietism... [Zionism is] the ideology, a ramified system of organisations and the practical politics of the wealthy Jewish bourgeoisie which has closely allied itself with monopoly circles in the USA and other imperialist countries."

## Context:
# Some Zionology books were included in mandatory reading lists for military and police personnel, students, teachers, and Communist Party members
# These definitions were mass published and formed the official Soviet framework for discussing Zionism/Israel
